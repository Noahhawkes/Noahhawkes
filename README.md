## Research commitments

- **Evidence before confidence.** Keep original records and citations available where authorized.
- **Corrections remain visible.** Preserve documented supersessions and disputes.
- **Candidate is not canon.** Research proposals and fiction are not validated scientific results.
- **The human keeps the keys.** Approval and publication remain human-governed.
- **Tests over promises.** Report what actually ran and what remains proposed.

**Collaborate:** Start with the [ORACLE research invitation](https://github.com/HawkesNest-LLC/Oracle/blob/main/RESEARCH_INVITATION.md).

*Research and fiction may be AI-assisted. No claim of AI consciousness, identity transfer or production deployment is implied by a project proposal.*

**The system keeps the receipts. The human keeps the keys.**
